# Spec 034 evidence (R1)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented, until its R2 parts are built: continuous production evaluation (US7, FR-023, FR-024), review queues (US8 scenarios 2 and 3, FR-025), comparisons and business outcomes (US9, FR-026, FR-027), the organization level of US10 and the optional evaluator worker of ADR-0015 (FR-028). Defaults, deviations and gaps of each R1 slice are in the spec 034 section of `docs/architecture/r1-implementation-notes.md`.
