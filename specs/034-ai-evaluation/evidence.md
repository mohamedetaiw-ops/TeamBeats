# Spec 034 evidence (R1, R2 in progress)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented, until its R2 parts are built: the optional evaluator worker of ADR-0015 (FR-028). R2 so far: continuous production evaluation (US7 scenarios 1 to 5, FR-023, T018), drift detection (US7 scenario 6, FR-024, T019), review queues (US8 scenarios 2 and 3, FR-025, T020), online comparisons (US9 scenarios 2 and 3, FR-026, T021) business outcomes (US9 scenario 1, FR-027, T022), the organization level (US10, FR-020, T023) and French, Urdu and Hindi cases (US1 scenario 6, T024). Defaults, deviations and gaps of each R1 slice are in the spec 034 section of `docs/architecture/r1-implementation-notes.md`, and of R2 in `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md`.
