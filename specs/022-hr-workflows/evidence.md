# Spec 022 evidence (R2)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented. Calendars (R1) and every R2 part are built: leave, HR balances and recount, reduced hours, team availability, attendance, journeys with pre-boarding, letters, data change and expenses with AI receipt reading. The R3 parts of the spec (probation and contract milestones, shift rosters, receipt reading for all tenants) are not built. The spec 022 section of `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md` and the plan hold the defaults, deviations and gaps. Test classes are in `tests/Integration` unless marked unit or e2e. Every e2e suite runs in English and Arabic with axe.
