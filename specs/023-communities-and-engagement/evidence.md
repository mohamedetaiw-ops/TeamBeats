# Spec 023 evidence (R2)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented. Every part of the spec is built: communities and posts with images and files, Speak Up, polls, surveys, recognition and points, awards, celebrations, galleries, moderation, home cards and analytics counts. The spec 023 sections of `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md` (slices 1 to 12) hold the defaults, deviations and gaps. Test classes are in `tests/Integration` unless marked unit or e2e. Every e2e suite runs in English and Arabic with axe.
