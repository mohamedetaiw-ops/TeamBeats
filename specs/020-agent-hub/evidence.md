# Spec 020 evidence (R1 and R2)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented, until its R3 part is built: handoffs between agents (FR-016). R2 closed the items left open from R1: forced security updates of templates (FR-005) and contextual actions configured per kind of item (FR-012). R2 also built the Studio, the marketplace, memory, daily briefings, the department templates, streamed answers with resume after restart (closing T004 and T005) and detailed analytics. Defaults, deviations and gaps are in the spec 020 and 027 section of `docs/architecture/r1-implementation-notes.md` and the spec 020 R2 sections of `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md`.
