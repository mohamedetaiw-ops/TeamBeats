# Spec 028 evidence (R2)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented. Every part of the spec is built: courses with versions, quizzes and paths, certificates, assignment rules with recurrence and grace, reminders and overdue escalation, onboarding and policy training, reports, the team view and the evidence export, the LMS connector, and SCORM 1.2 and xAPI packages. The spec 028 sections of `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md` (slices 1 to 6) hold the defaults, deviations and gaps. Test classes are in `tests/Integration` unless marked unit or e2e. The e2e suite runs in English and Arabic with axe.
