# Spec 029 evidence (R2)

The spec stays Approved, not Implemented. Everything in its R2 scope is built: project and team spaces with public, private and secret visibility, members and guests, task lists and the board, assignees, due dates, milestones, progress and status, the space library and discussion, task file links, the team calendar, templates, and My tasks in the task inbox. The R3 items (dependencies and timeline view, time tracking, Planner and Jira sync, AI status summaries) are not started. The spec 029 sections of `docs/architecture/r2-implementation-notes.md` (slices 1 to 4) hold the defaults, deviations and gaps. Test classes are in `tests/Integration` unless marked e2e. The e2e suite (`spaces.spec.ts`) runs in English and Arabic with axe.
