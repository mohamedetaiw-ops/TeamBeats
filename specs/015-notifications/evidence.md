# Spec 015 evidence (R1, R2)

Spec 015 is built for both releases it targets. R1 builds the send contract, the in-app centre, email over SMTP, preferences with mandatory categories exempt, grouping, the daily digest, quiet hours, templates per locale, delivery records, retries and bounces. Sending modules use it: critical announcements, content reviews and comments (009), documents (011), request updates and approvals (016), policy approvals and attestations (025), security alerts (026), agent owners and lifecycle (020, 027) and evaluation gates (034). R2 adds Teams activity notifications with email fallback and approve and reject from a Teams bot card (US5), web push (030), Slack direct messages (017) and SMS for mandatory messages (US6), signage screens of a location (US7) and delivery reports of critical announcements (US8). The R2 build slices in `plan.md` hold the defaults and the gaps; the spec 015 section of `docs/architecture/r1-implementation-notes.md` holds the R1 ones. Test names are in `tests/Integration` unless another suite is named.
