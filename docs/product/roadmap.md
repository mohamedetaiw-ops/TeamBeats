# Release roadmap

Adopted 2026-11-04 (Mohamed). Replaces the M/S/L priority labels in the foundation: each requirement is now due in a named release, recorded in its spec's "Target release" field.

| Release | Foundation phases | Contents | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R0 Internal alpha** | Phase 0 and 1 | Repo, CI, deployment skeleton. Tenant management, organization hierarchy, dev-only sign-in, authorization, entitlement core, audit and admin shell, localization framework (specs 001 to 007) | Cross-tenant and org boundary suites green; constitution checks automated in CI |
| **R1 Pilot** | Phase 2 and 3 (core) | Entra ID sign-in and Entra ID directory sync (users, groups, org mapping). Employee home, news and pages, directory and org chart, document library, search, AI Gateway with OpenRouter, cited AI assistant. Service catalogue with linked apps, simple form services and one task inbox (pending decision). Content approval, owners, review dates and critical announcements with acknowledgement; controlled documents and knowledge base; policy register with attestation and policy Q&A; tenant security policy (IP rules, session rules, MFA check, step-up). Arabic and English complete | First design-partner tenant live; dev sign-in absent from production artifact |
| **R2 General availability** | Phase 3 (rest), 4 and 6 | Multi-step workflows and service templates, BPMN and DMN process engine (compensation and migration in R3), leave, attendance and HR journeys (onboarding, offboarding, letters, expenses), communities and engagement, sites and page builder, campaigns across channels, retention and legal hold, brand asset portal, guest users and secure extranet spaces, SIEM streaming, compliance projects, learning, project spaces, mobile app, Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, HR sync and payslip, notifications, quotas and metering, Google, SAML and AD FS sign-in, on-premises Active Directory sync, analytics. French, Urdu and Hindi complete | Paid packages sellable; performance and penetration tests passed; ISO 27001:2022 certificate and SOC 2 Type I report in hand |
| **R3 Enterprise & Government** | Phase 5 | Agents with write tools, Oracle Fusion and SAP packs, dedicated and on-prem hardening, DR validation, SCIM, customer-managed keys, four-eyes admin changes, national government certifications, records disposition certificates, business KPI tiles on location dashboards | First on-prem government deployment |

Open: success measures per release (review finding B4).
